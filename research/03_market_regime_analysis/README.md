# Research 03: Market Regime Analysis

## 1. Overview

This research investigates how different cryptocurrency market regimes influence the performance of algorithmic trading strategy families.

The objective is to identify the market conditions under which individual strategy families perform effectively, understand their sensitivity to volatility and market direction, and evaluate whether market regime information can improve strategy selection and portfolio-level decision-making.

The analysis uses historical 30-minute market regime features and completed trades generated by the algorithmic trading platform.

The primary objectives are:

- Analyze the distribution of different market regimes.

## 5. Dataset Description

The research uses two primary datasets.

### Dataset A: Market Regime Features

**File:** `data/raw/market_regime_features_30m.csv`

Contains 30-minute market observations for all 50 trading pairs.

The dataset includes:

- OHLCV market data.
- Trend probability and trend strength.
- Trend direction and reversal probability.
- ADX and EMA-based trend indicators.
- ATR-based volatility measurements.
- Bollinger Band width and compression indicators.
- Volume and liquidity features.
- H2 and H8 higher-timeframe indicators and model predictions.
- Multi-timeframe trend alignment and confluence features.
- Market regime classification features.

Each record represents a market observation for a particular trading pair and timeframe.

### Dataset B: Strategy Trades

**File:** `data/raw/strategy_trades_all_families.csv`

Contains completed trades generated by all eight strategy families.

The dataset includes trade identification, symbol, strategy family, entry and exit timestamps, direction, prices, trade outcome and performance metrics.

The two datasets are aligned using the trading symbol and the latest market feature observation available at or before the trade entry time.

## 6. Market Regime Dimensions

The research evaluates market conditions across four dimensions.

### 6.1 Trend Regime

Market conditions are categorized into:

- **Trending:** Market exhibits directional movement and trend strength.
- **Ranging:** Market exhibits relatively limited directional movement.
- **Transition:** Market exhibits changing or uncertain directional conditions.

### 6.2 Market Direction

Market direction is analyzed through:

- Bullish
- Bearish
- Neutral, where applicable

### 6.3 Volatility Regime

Market volatility is evaluated using three categories:

- Low Volatility
- Normal Volatility
- High Volatility

### 6.4 Combined Market Regime

A combined regime represents the interaction between market direction, trend state and volatility.

Examples:

- Bullish / Trending / High Volatility
- Bearish / Trending / Normal Volatility
- Bullish / Ranging / Low Volatility
- Bearish / Transition / High Volatility

The exact classification rules and thresholds are documented in `methodology.md`.

## 7. Performance Evaluation Metrics

Each strategy family is evaluated using the following metrics:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Trades | Number of completed trades |
| Winning Trades | Number of profitable trades |
| Losing Trades | Number of unprofitable trades |
| Win Rate | Percentage of winning trades |
| Net P&L | Total net trading profit or loss |
| Profit Factor | Gross profit divided by gross loss |
| Average P&L | Average net P&L per trade |

Performance is evaluated at both market-regime and strategy-family levels.

Trade count is reported alongside performance metrics to avoid drawing conclusions from insufficient samples.

## 9. Methodology and Data Integrity

The research follows these principles:

1. Use historical market observations and completed trades from the defined analysis period.
2. Match each trade with the most recent eligible market observation available at entry.
3. Prevent future information from entering historical market features.
4. Ensure higher-timeframe indicators and predictions are available at the actual decision timestamp.
5. Apply consistent regime definitions across strategy families.
6. Report sample sizes alongside performance metrics.
7. Treat descriptive historical performance as evidence for further investigation, not proof of future profitability.

**Look-ahead bias prevention:** Market features must be timestamped according to when they became available, not merely when their underlying candle opened. The matching procedure must use only information available at the trade decision time.

Detailed data preparation, timestamp alignment, regime classification and metric calculations are documented in `methodology.md`.

## 10. Expected Research Findings

This research aims to establish:

- Which strategy families are more suitable for trending markets.
- Which strategy families are more suitable for ranging markets.
- Which strategies benefit from high or low volatility.
- Whether directional market conditions influence strategy performance.
- Whether certain combined regimes create favorable or unfavorable trading environments.
- Whether market regime information can support better strategy allocation and portfolio risk management.

These findings will provide an empirical foundation for future regime-aware strategy selection and portfolio optimization research.

## 11. Limitations

Historical performance does not guarantee future results.

The research may be affected by:

- Market structure changes.
- Cryptocurrency volatility and liquidity variations.
- Strategy-specific trade frequency.
- Small sample sizes in certain regime combinations.
- Feature timing and higher-timeframe data availability.
- Transaction costs and execution assumptions.
- Overfitting when interpreting historical regime-specific performance.

Any proposed regime-based strategy changes should be validated through out-of-sample and walk-forward testing before production deployment.

## 13. Conclusion

Market regime analysis provides a systematic approach to understanding the relationship between cryptocurrency market conditions and algorithmic strategy performance.

Rather than assuming that one strategy performs consistently across all market conditions, this research evaluates the effectiveness of individual strategy families under different market environments.

The ultimate objective is to use empirical market-regime evidence to support more informed strategy selection, allocation and portfolio-level risk management.
